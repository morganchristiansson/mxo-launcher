## Key specialized methods
- `0x0043b830` is the outgoing half: it verifies auth connectivity, builds a small auth packet whose first payload byte is `0x35`, sends it, and posts event `0x1b`.
- `0x0043d4d0` is the large incoming half: on opcode `0x36` success it copies world entries into owner-managed storage, normalizes invalid world status/type values, then switches to state `3` and posts follow-up events.
- Slot 7 (`0x00438ce0`) is another tiny state-id getter, returning `14`.
