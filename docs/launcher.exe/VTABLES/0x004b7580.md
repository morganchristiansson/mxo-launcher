## Key call-shape facts
Outer validation wrappers now close cleanly:
- `0x468f80 = AuthBootstrap680_VerifyReplyPublicKeyAgainstLazyPubkeyDatValidator`
  - builds exactly `0x81` signed bytes = `0x80` modulus bytes + exponent low byte
  - calls validator vtable `+0x2c(..., signatureBytes, 0x100)`
- `0x44ae40 = AuthBootstrapReplyCopyShadowF4_BuildSignedDataMd5Digest10`
  - builds MD5 over copied signed-data `this+0x80 .. +0x135` (`0xb6` bytes)
- `0x44aec0 = AuthBootstrapReplyCopyShadowF4_VerifyWithValidator`
  - first checks freshness with child `+0x80` bias and copied expiry dword `+0xac`
  - then calls validator vtable `+0x2c(md5Digest10, 0x10, this, 0x80)`

## Current fidelity boundary
The remaining uncertainty is inside the validator-worker family itself and/or the `qspubkey.dat` init/read path, not in:
- the signed-byte span for raw `0x07`
- the MD5 span for raw `0x0b`
- the freshness gate in `0x44add0`

That is why current source comments keep the diagnostic-only bypass sharply bounded instead of treating it as a generic crypto failure.
